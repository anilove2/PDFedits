Annex A

Method notes

Figures are rounded to one decimal place. Where a metric changed definition mid-quarter, the earlier period is restated.

Definitions

  1. Revenue is recognised when the service period starts.
  2. Retention counts accounts active in the last 28 days.
  3. Support load is tickets per 100 accounts.
